# Screen-by-screen audit, September 2026

Every DME screen opened in a real signed-in browser, compared against what a US
DMEPOS supplier actually has to do, and fixed.

**Result: 442 tests passing (was 323). 160 end-to-end checks passing, 0 failed.
0 build errors.**

The bar used for "needed": anything whose absence causes **lost money, a denial,
or a compliance failure**. Not convenience, not polish.

---

## The five that mattered

**1. The rental cap was never enforced on the server.**
`BillNow` had no cap check of any kind. The Rentals screen hid the button and
that was the entire protection, which a replayed form, a second tab or any
future API client walks straight past. A capped rental ends at 13 months and the
equipment becomes the customer's, so billing month 14 is billing for equipment
the supplier no longer owns. That is not a denial to appeal, it is a false
claim. Fixed, together with the double-bill: two people clicking Bill now
produced two claims for the same month.

**2. A customer could only ever be created, never edited.**
No edit action existed anywhere. A mistyped member ID or date of birth rejected
every claim for that person for as long as the record lived, and the only remedy
staff had was a duplicate customer record. There was no secondary insurance at
all, so a Medicare supplement could never be billed and its 20% went to the
patient instead. Built.

**3. Referring doctors could not be added.**
Three seeded rows in a dropdown. Every DMEPOS claim needs an ordering physician
in boxes 17 and 17b, and referrals come from whichever doctor the patient saw.
A fourth doctor's referral could not be processed without filing it under
somebody who never signed it. Built, with NPI check-digit validation.

**4. Three eligibility claims the product had never checked.**
A green "Eligible" chip painted unconditionally on every insurance row; a
"Validate Insurance" button that waited 1.1 seconds and printed "Active · DME
covered · Deductible met · 20% coinsurance" from a string literal; and a
database column written as the constant `'active'` and read by nothing.
"Deductible met" is the dangerous one: a biller who believes it does not collect
the patient's share, and routinely not collecting patient responsibility is an
inducement. All three removed.

**5. An Attachments panel that silently discarded documents.**
The New Customer screen accepted a CMN, listed it on screen with a remove
button, and threw it away on save. Undetectable from the outside: an upload that
reports success and stores nothing looks exactly like one that worked. Staff
would scan a certificate, believe it was on file, and have nothing when the
payer asked. Removed, and order documents gained the categories a DMEPOS audit
actually turns on.

## Two mistakes I made and caught

**An unclosed `<script>`.** Removing the dead Attachments block took the closing
tag with it, so the browser read the rest of the page as JavaScript and every
typeahead on the New Customer form died silently. Clean build, HTTP 200, correct
screenshot. Found by reading the browser console. `DmeViewMarkupTests` now counts
tags across every view.

**A parameterised DDL object name.** `ALTER TABLE ... DROP CONSTRAINT @c` inside
`sp_executesql` is a syntax error, and it fails at run time with a message about
the constraint rather than the parameter, so it reads like a dependency problem.
It was in two migrations and worked in the first only because that column had no
default. Both fixed and pinned.

## Not done, and why

**ERA / 835 ingestion.** Blocked on the client's clearinghouse account. Cannot
be built or tested without one. Thread 4.

**The cheque batch header.** RehabDox has `PaymentBatches`: one cheque, many
claims. DME posts one receipt per claim, so a cheque covering twelve rental
months is opened twelve times. This is the largest remaining gap for a working
biller and it needs no clearinghouse. Thread 8.

**What a customer owes.** `PatientResponsibility` is computed per claim line and
nothing rolls it up per customer, so nobody can see who owes what. `PR` money is
legally required to be chased. Thread 8.

**The orphaned Collect Payment modal.** 51 references in `GlobalBridge.js`
against two deleted endpoints, with no markup left to open it. Inert, separate
task. Thread 9.

**`RecordingService.js`.** Clinical audio recording, now inert because the
SignalR library it checked for is gone. Dead weight, not breaking anything.

## Demo data to fix before showing a client

None of these are code faults. All are one line of SQL.

1. **The three rentals are overdue since June.** The Dashboard correctly reports
   3 overdue, which is honest but not the story you want to open with.
2. **`CLM-02002` is "Ready to bill" and "Denied" at once.** The seed posted a
   denial against a claim that was never submitted, which cannot happen in life.
3. **Two serialised-unit rows are consumables**, left over from the bug fixed
   above. `DELETE FROM DmeSerializedUnits WHERE UnitId IN (8,9)`.
4. **Every NPI in the demo data fails its check digit**, including the
   supplier's own. Editing a doctor now refuses until it is corrected.
   `1234567893` and `1999999984` are valid examples.
5. **Tenant 1's `LogoUrl` points at a file that does not exist**, so every page
   load makes one 404. Handled correctly by the UI, but noisy.
